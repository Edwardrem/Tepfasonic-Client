Full screen without grey glass, and the app described as what it is: a DevOps and AIOps tool for fleets of servers.

### Fixed

- **Full screen with scheme-coloured chrome.** macOS 26 moves the toolbar into a window of its own in full screen and paints grey glass behind the title and behind the buttons (darker still while a button is pressed or its menu is open); no SwiftUI modifier reaches it. With the scheme on the chrome, Tepfasonic now hides the system toolbar in full screen and draws its own header rows: the sidebar toggle at the top of the sidebar (or at the left of the detail column while the sidebar is hidden), the window title and host at the left, and the Claude, Search and `+` buttons at the right, where they are in a window. Leaving full screen brings the system toolbar back. With the scheme off the system toolbar is kept as before.

### Changed

- The Claude panel's scope menu shows *All hosts* and each group with its host count, with icons, so a conversation with a group of servers is one pick away. (Group scope has been there since the panel; it only lists groups once you have some.)
- README, website and the GitHub repository describe Tepfasonic as a DevOps and AIOps tool for managing large fleets of servers.

### For developers

- `FullScreenToolbarSwitch` (App/Tepfasonic/Theming/FullScreenChrome.swift) tracks the window's full-screen state and toggles `toolbar.isVisible`; `FullScreenSidebarHeader` / `FullScreenDetailHeader` are the header rows and `ContentView` shares its toolbar controls between the toolbar and the header. `--qa-exit-fullscreen` leaves full screen again after the QA dump.
