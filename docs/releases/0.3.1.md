Groups you can shape like projects, and a top bar that keeps its colour in full screen.

### Added

- **Group management in the sidebar.** The `+` button's menu adds a host, a group or a serial console (click still adds a host). Right-click a group for *New Host in <group>…*, *Edit Group…*, *Connect to All*, *Move Up* / *Move Down* and *Delete Group…* (its hosts are kept and move back to Hosts; a group with hosts asks first). Groups collapse with the chevron and remember it. A group with no hosts says so and takes drops.
- **Drag and drop.** Drag a host onto a group header or between the rows of another group to move it; drag within a group to reorder. The drag carries a private type, so dropping a host on a terminal pastes nothing.
- **A host checklist in the group editor.** The editor lists every host with a checkbox and the group it belongs to today, so a project is assembled in one place. *Move to Group → New Group…* on a host opens the editor with that host already ticked, and the host editor's Group row has a `+` that creates a group and selects it.
- New hosts and groups land at the end of their list instead of the top.

### Fixed

- In full screen the toolbar lives in its own window and macOS paints a dark grey backdrop behind the window title there, which showed as a block on scheme-coloured chrome. The title is now hidden while the window is in full screen (the tab and the status bar name the host) and comes back when it leaves. The grey scroll-edge strip at the top of the sidebar, the tab bar and the Claude transcript in full screen is gone as well.

### For developers

- `HostGrouping` in `TepfaCore` owns moving hosts between groups, list reordering and renumbering, with tests; `HostDrag` in the app wraps the drag payload. `--window=new-group` and `--window=fullscreen` are new QA flags.
