A design pass over the whole app, the colour scheme carried into every surface, and files in and out of the Claude panel.

### Added

- **Attach files to Claude.** The paperclip in the composer, or a drop anywhere on the panel, attaches images (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP; HEIC and oversized images are converted and scaled), PDFs, Word documents (sent as their text) and text files such as logs, configs, code, CSV and JSON. Attachments show as chips on the message, with thumbnails for images, and go to the model before your words. The Anthropic provider sends real image and document blocks; OpenAI-compatible endpoints get data-URI images and extracted text.
- **Claude writes documents.** Ask for a report, summary, inventory or runbook "as a PDF" or "as a Word document" and the new `create_document` tool renders Claude's Markdown as a paginated PDF (title, date, headings, bullet and numbered lists, tables with a header row, code blocks, quotes, page numbers), a Word `.docx` (real heading styles, lists, tables, hyperlinks and a page-number footer), HTML, Markdown, plain text or CSV. Files are saved in `Agent/Documents` under the app's data folder and appear in the transcript as a card with Open, Show in Finder, Save As… and Copy Path. Nothing runs on a host for this.
- **The scheme colours the whole app.** Sidebar, tab bar, status bar, Claude panel, cards, pills, sheets and the backgrounds of every window (sessions, Files, Theme Editor, Identities & Keys, Settings) are derived from the colour scheme in use, so Solarized, Nord or Gruvbox look like themselves outside the terminal too. The accent becomes the scheme's blue (or cyan, green, magenta… whichever reads against the background) unless you pick one. Settings → Appearance → "Colour the whole app with the scheme" turns this off for the standard macOS materials.

### Changed

- **A quieter, iOS-flavoured interface.** Hosts and serial consoles get rounded icon tiles in their colour with the connection state in the corner; tabs are pills with the selected one raised; the status bar leads with a status capsule; the quick-connect field is a capsule with a clear button; the empty workspace shows one tile, one line and the four shortcuts that matter. The Claude panel uses message bubbles (yours in the accent colour, Claude's as plain text with real headings, lists, tables and code), chip-style scope and conversation pickers, suggestion cards when a conversation is empty, and a rounded composer with the attach and send buttons.
- Claude's replies render block-level Markdown (headings, lists, tables, fenced code, quotes) instead of a single inline run.
- The approval sheet and the new-tab picker use the same tiles and cards.

### Fixed

- The Theme Editor window opens wide enough for its list, form and preview; at its old size the sidebar and the preview buttons were cut off.

### For developers

- `AgentContentBlock.attachment`, `AgentAttachment`, `AttachmentImporter`, `MarkdownDocument`, `DocumentRenderer` (PDF through the Cocoa text system, DOCX as hand-written OOXML zipped with `ditto`) and `AgentDocumentReference` live in `TepfaAgent` with tests; `AppPalette` and the `Design` components live in the app.
- `--qa-agent-demo` seeds a finished conversation with attachments and a generated PDF for screenshots.
